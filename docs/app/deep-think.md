Pick the mode in the ask box. Roundtable tells you how long it takes before it asks.

## Quick

Quick is the default. It takes about 2 minutes.

- One round of short answers.
- A short verdict: the pick, how sure the table is, what would change it, three next steps and the case against.

Use Quick for most questions.

## Deep Think

Deep Think is for complex decisions. It takes about 14 minutes.

- The coach always sharpens your question first.
- The table argues for up to 4 rounds. Each round pushes against the leading pick.
- The chair writes a long verdict: the reasoning, the risks, the unknowns, a step plan and the full case against.

Deep Think works best with Claude connected, because Claude is the chair.

## When the coach suggests it

If your question looks complex, the coach says so after its questions. Click Use Deep Think, or Keep Quick.

The verdict card and the saved file both say which mode ran.
