A decision model reads the options the table argued. It gives each option a probability, plus "need more information". It says so when it disagrees with the table.

It is off unless you turn it on, in Settings, Decision model.

## The choices

- Off. You get the table's verdict only.
- Jev. TypeSafe's hosted decision model. It needs your Jev key.
- Jebadiah. Our free decision model. It runs on your computer, and nothing leaves your machine.
- Another System One model, such as LINK21. Enter its address and model id.

## Get Jebadiah

Settings, Decision model shows how to get Jebadiah for this computer.

1. Roundtable checks your memory and picks the right size.
2. With Ollama, it gives you one line to paste into Terminal. With LM Studio, it gives you the exact model id to download.
3. Roundtable then finds Jebadiah by itself. You can also enter its address.

Use the full frontier-infra id. Another account with a similar name holds unrelated models.

## Read the result

Under the verdict, a bar shows each option's probability. The probabilities are the decision model's, not the table's. When it picks a different option than the table, the panel says so in plain words.
