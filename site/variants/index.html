Which design for roundtable.sh?

Three real directions for the marketing site. Same copy and sections in each; only the brand and layout change. Open each page to scroll it in full.

Recommended: A, Chair pink. It keeps the pink chair and the table mark that the app, the icon and the docs already use, so nothing else has to change, and the table makes the product idea clear in one look.

AChair pink

Near black, one pink accent for the chair and the main button, Geist and Geist Mono, sharp corners. The hero is the round table with every AI seated; the seats light up in turn and the table reads Consensus.

  • Closest to the app's brand file, so app, icon and site match.
  • Dark and technical; fits developers and x.ai style AI tools.
  • Risk: dark plus pink is common among AI sites, so the table picture has to carry the identity.

BCobalt paper

Warm paper ground, cobalt ink, Host Grotesk set very large, a red chair in the mark. The hero is the live app window, cut at the fold. Big plain numbers (8, 4, 0) explain the product.

  • Stands out most from other AI tools; reads calm and editorial.
  • A real rebrand: the app, icon and docs would need the new colours.
  • Light page; the dark app screenshots would need light versions.

CForest lime

Deep forest green with a lime accent, Urbanist and DM Mono, rounded pill shapes. The hero shows one question fanning out to six AIs and coming back as one verdict, with pulses running along the lines.

  • Explains how Roundtable works on the first screen.
  • Warmer and friendlier than A; still dark, so app screenshots fit.
  • Also a rebrand: lime replaces pink everywhere.
