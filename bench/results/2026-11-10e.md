## How Quick was tuned

Five Quick runs of all 12 scenarios, same seats, single AI and judge. Only the last one is in the tables above. The raw files of runs 1 to 4 were kept off the repo.

| Run | Build | What changed | Quick total | Mean time |

| 3 | 3aca544 | The verdict adds risks with how to blunt them, and the unknowns | 32.1 | 79.5 s |

Run 5 is 1.0 below rc8 (33.5 rounded; 1.04 on the unrounded 33.54). That sits on the limit of "no more than 1 point". Time is 77% lower (358.3 s to 80.9 s). Lines lost against rc8: honest minority view (5.0 to 4.3) and flags what is unknown (4.7 to 4.2). Trade-offs went up (4.5 to 4.8). The judge is one model; a gap under 0.5 on a line is noise.

Deep Think was run on restructure and vendor-contract. It scored 34.0 and 34.5, against rc8's 32.5 and 34.0 and Quick's 30.0 and 30.5, in about 14 minutes each (800.8 s and 872.2 s). The app's estimate for Deep Think says about 14 minutes.
